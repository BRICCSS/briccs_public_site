Group leadership

Dr. Makoto Jones

BRICCS group leader

Dr. Jones leads BRICCS and directs the Biosurveillance, Antimicrobial Stewardship, and Infection Control Program within the VA's Clinical Assessment, Reporting, and Tracking (CART) Program.

As a health services researcher, infectious diseases physician, and informatician, he uses national VA electronic health record data to advance scientific discovery, healthcare quality improvement, biosurveillance, antimicrobial stewardship, and infection control.
