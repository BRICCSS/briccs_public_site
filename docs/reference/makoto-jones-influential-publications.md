# Makoto Jones: Influential Publications

> **Document purpose:** Source material for a selected-publications section on the BRICCS website.
> Metrics were retrieved from NIH iCite on **September 8, 2026** and will change over time.

## Selection method

The supplied [PubMed search](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=Jones+MM&cauthor_id=28566096)
uses the broad author string “Jones MM” and currently includes publications by unrelated authors.
For disambiguation, PubMed was queried for **Makoto Jones [Author]**, and the top-ranked records were
checked to confirm that NIH author metadata includes Makoto Jones or Makoto M. Jones.

Eligible publications were ranked by NIH **Relative Citation Ratio (RCR)**, with PubMed-indexed
citation count as a tie-breaker. RCR is field- and time-normalized: 1.0 represents the median
NIH-funded article in the same field and year. Recent papers and some publication types may not yet
have an RCR. Citation counts below reflect publications indexed by PubMed, not every citation source.
See the [NIH iCite field definitions](https://support.icite.nih.gov/hc/en-us/articles/9342283674907-Descriptions-of-Influence-Module-data-fields).
